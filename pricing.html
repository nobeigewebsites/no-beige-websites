NEED TO WORK TO A BUDGET?

LET'S ADJUST THE scope, not the standards.

I'll always recommend the version of your website that I genuinely think best fits your brief. But if that sits outside your current budget, that doesn't automatically mean the project is dead. We can look at what you actually need right now and find a more affordable route — whether that's fewer pages, simpler functionality or splitting the project into phases.

I'll always be clear about what's included, what's being left out and what can be added later. Cheaper means a smaller scope — not the same work quietly squeezed into a smaller price.

BEFORE YOU ASK

THE OBVIOUS questions.

IS HOSTING INCLUDED?

No, unless your quote specifically says otherwise. Hosting, domains and other ongoing third-party services are normally paid separately by you.

CAN THE PRICE CHANGE?

Your agreed project scope has an agreed price. If you later request additional pages, functionality or work, I'll tell you the cost before doing it.

DO I HAVE TO PAY EVERYTHING UP FRONT?

Nope. Standard website projects use a 40% / 30% / 30% payment structure.

HOW MANY REVISIONS ARE INCLUDED?

Standard custom website projects include two rounds of design revisions. Changes outside the agreed scope are charged separately.

WHAT IF I NEED SOMETHING WEIRD?

Excellent. Tell me what it is and I'll work out whether I can build it and what it will cost.

ARE THE PRICES FIXED?

The prices shown are starting points. Your quote confirms the actual price based on the size, complexity and requirements of your project.
