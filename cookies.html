INTRO

WHAT THIS POLICY COVERS

This Cookie Policy explains how No Beige Websites uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our Website.

It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy , which explains how we handle personal data more generally.

01

WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that can be stored on your device when you visit a website.

They can be used for things like remembering preferences, keeping a website functioning properly, understanding how visitors use a site, or supporting third-party services.

02

TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

ESSENTIAL TECHNOLOGIES

Some technologies may be necessary for the Website to function properly, provide security or remember essential Website behaviour.

PREFERENCE STORAGE

The Website stores your analytics consent choice in your browser so that you do not have to answer the cookie banner every time you visit a new page.

ANALYTICS COOKIES

No Beige Websites uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Website.

Analytics is only enabled after you choose to accept analytics through the cookie banner.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The Website also uses third-party services, including Google Analytics and Formspree.

These providers operate under their own privacy and data protection practices.

03

GOOGLE ANALYTICS

No Beige Websites uses Google Analytics to understand how people use the Website.

This can provide information such as which pages are visited, how visitors found the Website, general device or browser information, and how visitors move around the Website.

We use this information to understand what parts of the Website are useful, how people discover No Beige Websites and where the Website may be improved.

Analytics storage is denied by default. Google Analytics is enabled for analytics purposes only after you select ACCEPT ANALYTICS on the cookie banner.

No Beige Websites does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural advertising cookies through this Website.

04

CONTACT FORM & FORMSPREE

The contact form on this Website is provided using Formspree, a third-party form submission service.

Formspree processes the information you enter into the form in order to deliver your enquiry to No Beige Websites.

This may include your name, email address, business or project name, current website, project type, message and technical information associated with the submission.

Formspree may use technical information or similar technologies where necessary to provide its service, prevent abuse or protect the security of submissions.

More information about how personal data from contact forms is handled is available in our Privacy Policy .

05

COOKIE CONSENT

When you first visit the Website, you are given a choice about whether to allow analytics.

If you select ACCEPT ANALYTICS, your choice is stored in your browser and analytics storage is enabled.

If you select NO THANKS, analytics storage remains denied and your choice is remembered.

Technologies that are strictly necessary for the Website or for a service specifically requested by you may not require consent.

06

HOW TO CONTROL COOKIES

You can accept or reject analytics using the cookie banner when you first visit the Website.

Most web browsers also allow you to control or delete cookies and stored Website data through their settings.

You can usually choose to block cookies, delete existing cookies or control which websites are allowed to store information.

Blocking certain cookies or similar technologies may affect how parts of a website or third-party service function.

07

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

No Beige Websites uses third-party services to provide particular Website functions.

These currently include Google Analytics for Website analytics and Formspree for contact form submissions.

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy, security and cookie practices.

08

CHANGES TO THIS COOKIE POLICY

We may update this Cookie Policy when the Website changes, new tools are introduced or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

09
