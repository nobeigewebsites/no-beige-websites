01

WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that can be stored on your device when you visit a website.

They can be used for things like remembering preferences, keeping a website functioning properly, understanding how visitors use a site, or supporting third-party services.

02

TYPES OF COOKIES WE MAY USE

ESSENTIAL COOKIES

These are cookies that may be necessary for the Website to function properly.

They may support things such as navigation, security or basic Website functionality.

PREFERENCE COOKIES

These may remember choices you make, such as settings or preferences, so the Website can behave more consistently.

ANALYTICS COOKIES

If analytics tools are used in future, they may collect information about how visitors use the Website.

This can help us understand what pages are being used and where improvements may be needed.

THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

Some external services, embedded tools or platforms may use cookies or similar technologies.

These are controlled by the relevant third-party provider and subject to their own privacy and cookie practices.

03

WHAT WE CURRENTLY USE

The Website is currently designed to operate with minimal use of cookies.

At the time of this policy, No Beige Websites does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.

We do use Formspree to process enquiries submitted through the Website contact form.

When you submit the contact form, your information is transmitted through Formspree so that the enquiry can be delivered to No Beige Websites.

Formspree may use technical information, cookies or similar technologies where necessary to provide its service, prevent abuse or protect the security of form submissions.

HOW TO CONTROL COOKIES

Most web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings.

You can usually choose to block cookies, delete existing cookies or control which websites are allowed to store them.

Blocking certain cookies or similar technologies may affect how parts of a website or third-party service function.
