WHY WE'RE DOING IT

DOING GOOD SHOULDN'T mean blending in.

Small independent charities do some bloody incredible work.

They also don't usually have the budgets, teams or resources of the massive organisations.

Which means the website can become the thing that gets pushed to the bottom of the list.

And that's a problem.

Because a website isn't just there to look pretty. It can help people discover a charity, understand what it does, ask for help, volunteer, donate, support a campaign or simply realise that somebody out there gives a damn.

Brilliant work shouldn't have to whisper just because the budget is small.

WHO ARE WE LOOKING FOR?

SMALL. INDEPENDENT. DOING SOMETHING GOOD.

Make Good Loud is for small independent charities and not-for-profit organisations that could genuinely benefit from a stronger online presence.

Maybe they're doing incredible work locally. Maybe they're run by a tiny team. Maybe most of their money quite rightly goes into helping people rather than making their website look fancy.

They don't need to have the worst website on the internet to qualify.

What they're doing matters more than how ugly their current homepage is.

And yes — charities can nominate themselves. No imaginary friend called Susan required.

SO... WHAT'S THE CATCH?

THERE ISN'T ONE.

No competition entry fee. No design fee. No "free website" that suddenly costs £900 once somebody reads the small print.

When No Beige has the capacity, we'll choose a nominated charity and offer them a custom website design and build for free.

The only costs that may remain are unavoidable third-party costs such as a domain name, paid services or anything else the charity chooses to add that isn't part of the agreed build. We'll always make that clear before anything starts.

That's it. Good people doing good things. We make them louder.
